# Culver's Menu Prices, Calories and Nutrition — An Independent Guide

This independent guide explains how to think about Culver's menu prices, calories and nutrition before you order. It is not the official Culver's website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Culver's. Prices and nutrition information can vary by location, season and menu changes, so always check the current information at your chosen restaurant before deciding.

Culver's is best known for its ButterBurgers, fresh frozen custard and Wisconsin cheese curds. A ButterBurger is a beef burger served on a lightly buttered, toasted bun, and it can be ordered plain or with cheese, bacon, mushrooms and other toppings. The choice of toppings, patty size and sides is one of the main reasons the final price and calorie count of a meal can differ so much from one visit to the next.

## How prices usually work

Menu prices at a franchise restaurant are usually set with local costs in mind, including ingredients, labour, rent and local taxes. That is why the same burger or custard may cost more in one town than in another, and why a meal with a drink and fries costs more than a single sandwich on its own. Value meals, larger sizes and extra toppings also change the total, so it helps to compare the whole meal, not just one item.

## Calories and nutrition in plain terms

Calories are a measure of energy, and a richer meal with cheese, bacon, fried sides and sweet custard will usually contain more energy than a simpler meal. Nutrition is about more than calories alone. Protein, fat, saturated fat, sodium, sugar and fibre all matter. Frozen custard, for example, is a dessert, so it is best thought of as an occasional treat.

To keep a visit lighter, many people choose a smaller burger, skip an extra cheese or bacon topping, share a dessert, or pick a side in a smaller portion. Others prefer to enjoy the full meal and balance it with lighter meals earlier or later in the day. Your appetite, dietary needs, allergies and budget all matter, and restaurant staff can answer questions about ingredients and allergens.

## Planning your visit

Before you go, it can help to look at the menu in advance and decide whether you want a burger, chicken, fish, salad or custard. If you are comparing options, look beyond the headline price. Check what is included, whether a drink or side is part of the meal, and how the nutrition information fits your goals.
